Dribbling Madness Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Dribbling Madness ("the game") is a basketball tapping and trivia game. This policy explains what data the game handles. The short version: your game progress stays on your device. We have no servers and no user accounts.

Data stored on your device

Your score, upgrades, achievements, settings and other game progress are saved only on your device (and in your device's iCloud or computer backup, if you have backups turned on). We cannot see, access or recover it. Settings → Reset all data deletes it, and deleting the game removes it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your lifetime score to Apple's Game Center leaderboard. Game Center is run by Apple, and Apple's privacy policy applies to it. You can sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings at any time.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob. An ad plays only when you tap a button to watch one for an in-game bonus (for example, doubling the points you earned while away). Ads never appear on their own.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (including the Advertising Identifier, only if you allow tracking), IP address, general location derived from IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data. Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy (see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services).

You may be asked:

  • For your consent choices (players in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states), through Google's consent form. You can change these at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Whether to allow tracking, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are not personalized. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Ad content is limited to Google's PG content rating.

Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you tap Share (for your progress or an achievement). The text then goes through the iOS share sheet only to the destination you choose, and that destination's privacy policy applies.

Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases and never asks for payment information.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game itself collects no personal information; the only data collected is by Google for ads, as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
