Common questions

Where is my data stored? Only on your iPhone and in your device backups. There are no accounts and nothing is uploaded.

I got a new phone. How do I get my reports back? Restore your new phone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one.

How do I restore Pro? Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I delete my data? Delete a single property from its screen, or use Settings → Delete all data.
